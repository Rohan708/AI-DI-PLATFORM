Every database has problems nobody wrote down.

Hidden links

Tables point to each other through columns like cust_no or ITEM_CD, but nobody declared it. So nothing checks it.

Silent breakage

A nightly load runs halfway, a form stops saving postal codes, a migration drops a key. Dashboards keep showing numbers, just wrong ones.

Nobody knows normal

No documentation, no tests, and the person who built it left. You can't alert on "unusual" if you don't know what usual is.

How it works

Every night, four steps.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Reads the structure and finds the relationships nobody declared, from names, matching values and the app's own queries.

  2. 2

    Learn normal

    Learns each column's usual empties, ranges and values, and each table's weekday rhythm, from its own history.

  3. 3

    Find

    Checks eight kinds of problems. One digest, only what's new, with the rows involved.

  4. 4

    Explain

    What it means, what it breaks downstream, and what to check first. Every number is from the evidence.

AI discovers. People approve. Lumida enforces.

What it finds

Eight kinds of problems, every night.

Structure

Missing keys, dropped columns, changed types, slow unindexed links.

Relationships

Orphan rows and duplicate entities, even where no foreign key was declared.

Values

Sudden empties, impossible numbers, "USA" creeping in next to "US".

Business rules

"Shipped after ordered", "total = sum of lines". AI proposes, you approve.

Freshness & volume

Loads that didn't run or ran halfway, aware of weekdays and closed days.

Unusual rows

An order line with quantity 500 where 1–3 is normal, with its row IDs.

Copies that don't match

The warehouse copy is missing 7 orders the app database has.

Personal data

A map of where it lives, and a flag where it shouldn't be.

Security & privacy

Read-only. Inside your network. Your data stays put.

Read-only

Every query runs in a read-only transaction with statement and lock timeouts. Lumida never writes to your database.

Counted in place

All the counting happens inside your database. Only counts, statistics and the IDs of offending rows come back.

No values out

Row values aren't logged or sent anywhere. The optional AI features see structure and statistics, never your rows.

Self-hosted

Runs as a container inside your network, next to your database. Nothing to open to the internet.
